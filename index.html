I'm a Senior Engineer at Dynaspede Integrated Systems, Hosur, specializing in LabVIEW-based test automation and embedded systems. Since 2018, I've designed and deployed test rigs for clients across automotive, defence, and EV sectors.

My work covers the full stack of industrial test automation — NI hardware (cRIO, PXI, DAQ), Siemens PLC and drive integration, protocol bridging (PROFINET, MODBUS, CAN), and data management with SQL databases.

I specialize in writing LabVIEW GUI software that communicates reliably with complex industrial hardware — ECUs, VFDs, PLCs, power analyzers, and custom SDR platforms.
